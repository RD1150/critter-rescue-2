# App Store Launch and Plush Companion Plan

> **Financial planning note:** This is a pre-launch scenario model, not a sales guarantee or a recommendation to commit capital. Actual purchases will depend on price, product-page quality, reviews, discoverability, marketing, market fit, geography, and the family’s willingness to pay.

## Purchase Forecast Basis

The model uses **first-time paid app units** for a paid-upfront App Store release. It does not estimate subscriptions, in-app purchase conversion, refunds, Apple proceeds, advertising spend, or physical-product sales. Apple’s App Store Connect provides the right post-launch funnel measurements—impressions, product-page views, conversion rate, downloads, and sales—to replace these initial assumptions with observed performance.[1]

| Illustrative 90-day scenario | Assumed qualified App Store product-page views | Assumed paid-app conversion | Derived first-time purchases | Average purchases per month |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cautious | 500 | 2% | 10 | 3.33 |
| Base planning case | 2,500 | 4% | 100 | 33.33 |
| Upside | 10,000 | 6% | 600 | 200.00 |

The formula is **product-page views × paid-app conversion = first-time purchases**. The conversion assumptions are inputs for planning sensitivity, not industry benchmarks or observed Critter Rescue data. In practical terms, the responsible launch target is to validate the **base-planning case of 100 paid purchases in the first 90 days**, then reset the model every week from actual App Store Connect data.

## Decision Dashboard for the First 90 Days

| Week | What to measure in App Store Connect | Decision use |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Impressions, product-page views, first-time paid units, refunds | Validate whether families understand the store listing and price. |
| 3–4 | Conversion by source and custom product page, plus early review themes | Improve the icon, screenshots, parent-value promise, and age-appropriate store copy before scaling acquisition. |
| 5–8 | Conversion trend and regional performance | Continue only the channels that bring qualified parent interest, not raw impressions. |
| 9–12 | Paid units, refunds, active engagement, and any voluntary parent interest in companion products | Decide whether the app itself has sufficient repeatable demand to justify physical-product sampling. |

## Adult-Directed Plush Companion Strategy

The plushies should be **optional companion products**, not prizes and not child-facing sales hooks. The app can help a child care about Ember, Clover, Nutty, Sage, Shadow, and Summit; any purchase information should be in a clearly separate grown-up area or external parent marketing page. Do not use random rewards, scarcity, countdowns, locked learning, or prompts aimed at a child to generate a sale.

| Stage | Recommended action | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|
| Validate interest | Add a parent-only “companion plush updates” waitlist or a no-charge interest form outside the child flow. | Do not order inventory until interest is measurable and sourceable. |
| Prototype responsibly | Obtain physical samples from a specialist plush manufacturer and assess character resemblance, durability, materials, packaging, and landed cost. | Do not sell samples intended for children unless the complete compliance path is documented. |
| Confirm safety and compliance | Engage qualified counsel, a test lab, and the manufacturer on the applicable target-market requirements before marketing or sale. | In the U.S., children’s toys are subject to mandatory ASTM F963 requirements; products designed or intended primarily for children 12 and under have additional third-party testing and Children’s Product Certificate requirements.[2] |
| Limited launch | Launch an adult-directed, transparent fixed-price companion offer only after compliance and fulfillment are ready. | Use real unit economics, actual interest, and refund/return information to decide whether to scale. |

## Recommendation

Treat the app as the first product and the plush as a **second, separately validated product line**. Build the app’s paid demand and parent trust first. If the first 90 days approach or exceed the base planning case with healthy refund feedback and repeatable acquisition, commission compliant samples and test a parent-only companion offer. If actual conversion falls below the cautious case, improve positioning and the parent value proposition before taking inventory risk.

## Reference Date, Sources, and Confidence

The model was prepared on **August 28, 2026**. The scenario arithmetic is derived from explicit planning assumptions, so confidence in the numerical purchase outcomes is **low until Critter Rescue has observed App Store data**. Confidence is high that Apple’s listed analytics metrics are the appropriate funnel measurements and that a U.S. children’s plush line needs formal product-safety diligence.
