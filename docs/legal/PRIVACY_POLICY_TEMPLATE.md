## 2. Personal information we collect

We collect the categories below when they are relevant to your use of the Service.

### Account, identity, and workspace information

We may collect your name, email address, account identifier, authentication/session information, organization or coaching-business name, portal name, role, website, branding preferences, and account settings. Authentication may be provided through an identity provider. We receive the identity information that the provider is authorized to share with us.

### Coaching workspace and client content

The Service may store information that a coach or client enters or uploads, including a client’s name and email address; goals; target dates; commitments; constraints; available time; completed actions; obstacles; capacity or readiness selections; check-in responses; coach notes; route, milestone, and action records; reroute selections; and related workspace communications. Do not enter information that you do not have the right to provide.

VisionRoute is not designed to provide medical diagnosis, treatment, clearance, rehabilitation, emergency support, or other health-care services. **[Confirm whether you will prohibit health information and whether this product is subject to any health-privacy law before publishing.]**

### AI-related inputs and outputs

When an authorized user uses AI-assisted features, we may process the relevant workspace context to generate structured route suggestions, check-in summaries, reroute options, or coach briefs. The Service is designed to label AI interpretation separately from source information and to require coach approval for consequential changes. AI output can be inaccurate or incomplete and should be reviewed by a qualified human before use.

### Payment and transaction information

If paid plans are enabled, our payment processor may collect payment-card and billing information directly. We receive limited billing information, such as subscription status, plan, payment-processor customer identifier, and transaction or invoice references. We do not intend to store full payment-card numbers on VisionRoute systems.

### Device, usage, and security information

We may collect log, device, browser, IP-address, session, security, and activity information, such as sign-in events, workspace activity, feature use, audit events, and error diagnostics. We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.

### Communications
