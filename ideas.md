## Selected Direction — Hearthlight Field Journal

### Design Movement

**Contemporary nature journaling fused with tactile children’s-book illustration.** The experience should feel like opening a well-loved rescue notebook at a forest camp: crafted rather than polished, with evidence of a real caretaker’s map marks, stitched tabs, and thoughtful records.

### Core Principles

1. **Care feels competent.** The player is a gentle field rescuer, with clear missions and reassuring feedback rather than time pressure or danger.
2. **Tactility earns attention.** Paper grain, leaf silhouettes, rounded wood, wax-seal accents, and stitched borders make interactions feel physically meaningful.
3. **Progress is displayed as a living journal.** Camp upgrades, rescued critters, badges, and zone access appear as pages, pins, discoveries, and trail layers—not abstract counters.
4. **Warm asymmetry guides play.** UI uses trail-sign clusters, offset mission cards, and an open map surface rather than centered dashboard geometry.

### Color Philosophy

The world starts with **mossy forest darks, parchment warmth, and pale sky light** so the player enters a safe, outdoorsy place rather than a high-saturation toy interface. Rescue moments use a small dose of ownable **Foxglove Coral** to identify actions, saved critters, and celebration beats. This contrast makes active care visible without overwhelming the woodland palette.

### Layout Paradigm

The main experience is an **open field-journal spread**. The central map is the working page; a compact upper ribbon holds the day’s essentials, while an offset left trail-card and right pocket panel provide missions, rescue inventory, and navigation. Mobile collapses the satellite panels into thumb-reachable drawers while preserving the map as the primary canvas.

### Signature Elements

1. A **compass-and-paw trail marker** repeated in loading, buttons, and the rescue waypoint.
2. **Stitched paper edge and torn-note cards** for missions, narrative prompts, and saved critter entries.
3. **Hand-painted trail glyphs**—acorn, feather, paw, blossom, creek—to identify zones without relying only on text.

### Interaction Philosophy

Every interaction should imply kind, physical care: buttons press into paper, mission markers settle into the map, and completion messages arrive like a field note tucked under a leaf. Critical controls remain unambiguous and accessible; ornament never obscures a choice or result.

### Animation

Motion should be brief and organic. Paper cards lift by 2–4px on hover, map pins gently bob at rest, and successful rescues trigger a soft pop plus a short drifting-leaf trail. Scene entrances should fade and rise within 220–360ms using a custom ease-out. Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling non-essential bobbing and particle movement.

### Typography System

**Fraunces** provides a warm, subtly whimsical display voice for zone names, momentous rewards, and journal headings. **Nunito Sans** handles instructions, counts, and buttons with high legibility. Use sentence-case UI labels, strong numeric hierarchy, and no all-caps blocks longer than compact metadata.

### Brand Essence

**Critter Rescue is a calm woodland adventure for young-hearted caretakers who want every small act of help to leave a visible, hopeful mark.**

Personality: **nurturing, observant, quietly adventurous**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines and CTAs should sound like an encouraging field companion: specific, warm, and action-oriented without being saccharine. Microcopy recognizes effort and gives the player a meaningful next step.

> “A small pawprint needs a steady guide.”

> “Pack your kit and follow the feather trail.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a simple **coral compass rose whose center is a pawprint**, bordered by four short hand-inked trail ticks. It works as a one-color stamp and stays recognizable at favicon size. The wordmark, when used, pairs Fraunces in a custom arched lockup with the small compass-paw symbol.

### Signature Brand Color

**Foxglove Coral — `#E66B5B`**. This is reserved for rescue action, important earned progress, and the compass-paw mark.
