## Modeling Position

Critter Rescue is a **private, pre-launch, consumer preschool game**. It is not yet a subscription product and has no observed subscriber, churn, retention, cost, or profitability data. The forthcoming model will therefore use **gross subscription billings before platform fees, refunds, taxes, marketing, hosting, support, or transaction costs**. It will show the number of active paid subscribers needed for illustrative enterprise-value targets at clearly stated price and recurring-revenue multiple assumptions.

The critical buyer evidence is not subscriber count alone. A buyer would need to see active paid subscribers, monthly recurring revenue (MRR), annual recurring revenue (ARR), refund behavior, churn and retention cohorts, acquisition cost, gross margin, parent-support burden, documented asset rights, privacy compliance, and operations that are not dependent on a single founder.
