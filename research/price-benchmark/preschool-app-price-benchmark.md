# Critter Rescue — US Preschool-App Price Benchmark

**VERIFIED FACT — Scope:** This is a point-in-time US benchmark observed **October 9, 2026**, using the five supplied structured research results only. It is **not** a market average, a forecast, or a checkout quote. Storefront, platform, promotion, tax, eligibility, and future pricing can differ.

## VERIFIED FACT — Interpretation limits

- **VERIFIED FACT:** The recurring benchmarks above span **$7.99/month** (Sago Mini World), **$9.99/month** (Pok Pok), and **$14.99/month** (ABCmouse Premium); Lingokids’ observed promotional annual plan is **$71.88/year**. These figures are different billing structures and should **not** be averaged or treated as interchangeable. [1] [2] [5] [7] [8]
- **VERIFIED FACT:** The only supplied one-time content benchmark is Toca Boca World’s displayed **$1.99** optional pack; it is not evidence of a whole-app price. Its displayed individual IAPs run from $1.99 to $5.99. [6]
- **VERIFIED FACT:** The supplied comparables address young-child audiences; four cited price points are recurring access to growing content libraries or curricula. Toca Boca World instead uses a free-entry, à-la-carte content model. [1] [2] [5] [6] [7] [8]
- **VERIFIED FACT:** The supplied research characterizes Critter Rescue as a focused, calm, local-first rescue game for ages 3–6, rather than an account-based, continually renewing content library. This is why subscription list prices are useful context but are not a like-for-like purchase price.

## Initial Critter Rescue pricing

> **RECOMMENDATION — If Critter Rescue is sold as a permanent local-first app or one-time full unlock:** test an initial US price range of **$3.99–$5.99**, with **$4.99** as the preferred first test price.

**VERIFIED FACT — Benchmark rationale:** Toca Boca’s cited item is a **single $1.99 add-on** and the observed displayed individual-IAP band is $1.99–$5.99. The cited recurring library subscriptions begin at $7.99/month and extend to $14.99/month. [1] [2] [5] [6] [7]

**RECOMMENDATION — Why $4.99:** For a complete, focused preschool game, it is a meaningful one-time step above a single add-on while remaining below the first month of the observed library subscriptions—without implying that Critter Rescue offers an equivalent ongoing catalogue.

> **RECOMMENDATION — Position this as a one-time purchase, not a subscription:** offer a $4.99 paid app/full unlock for the available on-device game under the stated local-first premise. In contrast, the benchmark subscriptions renew for continued full-library or update access; their monthly or annual prices should not be presented as Critter Rescue’s direct price comparison.

**VERIFIED FACT — Subscription distinction:** Sago Mini World, Pok Pok, ABCmouse, and Lingokids use recurring subscription access in the supplied research; Toca Boca World is a free download with optional à-la-carte content purchases. [1] [2] [5] [6] [7] [8]

**Decision boundary:** This recommendation deliberately does not infer a market-wide “average,” a willingness-to-pay result, or a guaranteed optimum. Before launch, confirm the actual storefront fee model, platform-specific tax treatment, parental-gate approach, and whether future content promises would change the local-first/one-time proposition.
