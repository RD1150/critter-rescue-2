# Toca Boca World — US price benchmark

**Observed:** 2026-10-09
**App:** Toca Boca World: Game & Play (Toca Boca AB)
**Category:** Digital dollhouse and creative play

Toca Boca does not state a dollar price for Toca Boca World on the official product page reviewed. The current **US Apple App Store** listing—used as the price source because no company price page was found—shows the app download as **Free** and shows **“Strange Masquerade Style Pack” at $1.99** in its In-App Purchases section. This $1.99 is the single current US paid-price benchmark: it is an **optional, one-time in-app content pack**, not a subscription. The same listing also displays other individual IAPs (for example, $1.99, $2.99, $3.99, $4.99, and $5.99), so it should not be read as a price for all content or for a fully unlocked app. [1]

**Billing period / trial / promotion:** No billing period applies to the displayed $1.99 pack; it is presented as an individual IAP. No trial is stated. The pack is seasonal in name and its ongoing availability or price is not guaranteed; Toca Boca’s terms say IAP price and availability may change, including through limited-time offers and geographic variation. [1] [2]

**Access-model comparison:** Toca Boca World is free-to-download, single-player, and ad-free, with optional à-la-carte content purchases to expand the world. Its store description says there are no third-party ads and that new packs and locations are added to the in-app shop. [1] A calm, ad-free, local-first preschool rescue game for ages 3–6 would align with Toca Boca World on the no-third-party-ad and solo-play characteristics, but differs if it is designed to work locally/offline and/or offers access without an expanding in-app shop. It should not be described as equivalent in audience: Apple rates Toca Boca World 4+ and labels it “Made for Ages 6–8,” while saying its touch controls suit all reading abilities. [1]

**Uncertainty:** Apple’s public US listing is a point-in-time storefront display and may vary by platform, region, tax treatment, item availability, or later promotion. The official Toca Boca pages reviewed describe the app and its IAP rules but do not publish a fixed US dollar menu. No claim is made about market-wide pricing or about the rescue game’s own price, which was not provided.
