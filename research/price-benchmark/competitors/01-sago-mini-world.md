# Sago Mini World — US price benchmark

**Observed 2026-10-09:** **US$7.99 per month** for the **Sago Mini World Plan** on the US Apple App Store. This is the single benchmark recorded here. The company’s subscription FAQ says its current app prices are available through Apple, Google Play, Amazon, and its web store; its terms state that Sago Mini World is offered as a **monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription**. Apple’s US listing publicly identifies the Sago Mini World Plan at **$7.99**, but its visible purchase list does not itself label that specific entry “monthly.” Therefore the monthly price-period pairing has **medium confidence**, supported by the current Apple plan price and the company’s stated monthly/annual plan structure rather than a storefront line that combines both in one label. [1] [2] [3]

The app is free to download and subscription-gated. Apple says a subscription auto-renews unless renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and it can be cancelled through account settings. Sago Mini’s terms say subscriptions require an account and that a longer-than-one-month commitment is charged in full when purchased. [2] [5]

**Trial/promotion:** the official product page currently promotes “Get your free month.” The US Apple listing says to download to start a free trial but does not state its length. The US Google Play listing instead says new subscribers receive a free trial and should cancel before **seven days** to avoid a charge. This may reflect a channel-specific offer or stale/inconsistent copy; do not treat a universal one-month or seven-day trial as established. No purchase, enrollment, or checkout was performed. [1] [2] [4]

**Access-model comparison:** Sago Mini World is a subscription library of open-ended preschool play for **ages 2–6**, with hundreds of activities in one app. It says downloaded games can be played offline, one subscription can work across multiple devices, and subscribers receive no ads or in-app purchases. It is therefore comparable to a calm, ad-free preschool rescue game in child-directed, low-pressure play and offline-after-download use: Sago says it has no rules, time limits, or points. It is not fully comparable to a **local-first** access model, because the product is an account-based, auto-renewing membership whose access is tied to continued subscription rather than a stated permanent local entitlement. The Google Play storefront’s overall “Contains ads” label should not be read as contradicting Sago’s narrower promise of no ads **for subscribers**. [1] [2] [3] [4]

**Caveat:** This memo intentionally reports only one active US price benchmark. Other Apple in-app-purchase entries and annual options appear on the listing, including a separately named holiday annual sale, but are not normalized, selected, or presented as additional benchmarks. Prices, trial offers, and product availability can vary by storefront and change over time.
