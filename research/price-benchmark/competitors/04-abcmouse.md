# ABCmouse — US price benchmark

**Selected current US benchmark (one price):** **$14.99 USD per month plus applicable tax** for **ABCmouse Premium**. This is a recurring monthly subscription that auto-renews each month until cancelled. The price is an official company-stated USD price, observed **2026-10-09**. [1] [2]

**Trial / promotion:** The monthly Premium plan has a **30-day free trial for web sign-ups at ABCmouse.com**. ABCmouse says the trial is for new subscribers, requires a valid payment method, and converts to $14.99/month plus applicable tax unless cancelled; app-store sign-ups may have different trial terms. This makes the trial promotional and channel/eligibility dependent, rather than part of the recurring benchmark price. [1] [2]

**Access model and product fit:** ABCmouse is a broad, structured early-learning curriculum for **ages 2–8** (toddler through second grade), with 13,000+ activities across subjects and learning paths. Premium gates unlimited access behind the auto-renewing subscription; a separate Basic tier is free but limited to up to 10 activities daily. ABCmouse describes both tiers as safe and ad-free. [1] [3]

Compared with a **calm, ad-free, local-first preschool rescue game for ages 3–6**, the overlap is the child-directed, ad-free preschool digital experience. The access model is materially different: the ABCmouse benchmark is a renewable account subscription for an expansive curriculum, while the rescue-game concept is local-first and narrower in age range and activity type. This comparison does **not** assume a price, entitlement duration, or offline capability beyond the stated local-first positioning for Critter Rescue.

**Confidence and caveat:** **High confidence** in the selected $14.99/month price because two current official ABCmouse pages state it directly. Pricing is nevertheless channel- and offer-sensitive: taxes are additional, the web free trial may not apply in app stores or to prior subscribers, and ABCmouse says prices may change. Verify the displayed checkout terms before a real purchase; no purchase or enrollment was made for this research. [1] [2]
