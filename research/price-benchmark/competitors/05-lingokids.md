# Lingokids — US price benchmark

**Observed:** 2026-10-09
**Item:** Lingokids (rank 5) · preschool learning subscription

## Current US website benchmark

**$71.88 per year, charged annually after a 7-day free trial** for the active **Yearly Plan** web promotion. Lingokids displays this as **$5.99/month** and says billing recurs yearly until cancellation; the page also labels the offer a 55% saving. This is the single benchmark used here: it is an **up-front annual recurring subscription**, not a $5.99 monthly charge.[1]

Lingokids’ current official pricing support page separately gives its website example/standard rate as $13.49 per month or $161.88 per year after the 7-day trial. That page says exact price depends on platform, country, and any running offer, and directs customers to checkout for the applicable price.[2] The active $71.88 annual web offer is therefore the most relevant observed US website benchmark, but not a promise of the price on every device or at a later date.

## Access-model comparison

Lingokids serves ages **2–8**, so it overlaps the rescue game’s ages **3–6** preschool audience.[3] It is a broad learning-and-entertainment library: the paid Plus tier unlocks 4,000+ games, songs, videos, and activities, plus offline downloads, up to four profiles, progress tools, and screen-time limits.[4]

Like the proposed **calm, ad-free, local-first preschool rescue game**, Lingokids states that both its free Basic and paid Plus plans are ad-free.[5] The access models differ: Lingokids offers a free Basic tier limited to 10 refreshed activities per day, while Plus is an automatically renewing monthly or yearly subscription for full, unlimited access.[5] Plus does support **offline play after activities are downloaded**, but that is not the same as establishing a local-first product model; the company describes downloading content while online for later no-connection play.[4] The rescue game’s stated local-first design is therefore more directly oriented to on-device access, whereas Lingokids is a recurring, account-based library with an offline-download feature.

**Uncertainty:** This observation is limited to Lingokids’ US-dollar website offer on 2026-10-09. App Store, Google Play, Amazon, and Huawei prices can differ, and promotions can change. No checkout was completed and no subscription was purchased.[1] [2]
